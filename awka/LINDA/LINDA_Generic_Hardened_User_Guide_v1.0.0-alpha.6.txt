LINDA GENERIC HARDENED USER GUIDE
Version 1.0.0-alpha.6
Compatible with AWKA Kernel v1.0.0-alpha.4

======================================================================
1. PURPOSE OF THIS GUIDE
======================================================================

This guide explains how to install, configure, and operate the generic hardened
LINDA Job Report Domain Runtime.

LINDA manages an interpreting Job Report from source intake through:

- source registration and approval;
- request capture and clarification;
- assignment planning and readiness;
- service-delivery recording;
- post-assignment reporting;
- validation and finalization;
- controlled export, versioning, restoration, and closure.

The generic release contains no organization-specific identity. The implementing
organization supplies its authorized configuration during installation. That
configuration controls branding and organizational defaults only. It does not
constitute evidence for a particular Job Report.

LINDA runs as a Domain Runtime on AWKA. AWKA must be bootstrapped and READY
before LINDA is activated.

======================================================================
2. RELEASE COMPONENTS
======================================================================

The generic package uses three uploaded executable/declarative components and
one message-delivered installation directive.

Upload exactly these three files:

1. 001_LINDA_Generic_Manifest_v1.0.0-alpha.6.json
2. 002_LINDA_Generic_Model_v1_0_0_alpha_6.py
3. 003_LINDA_Generic_Verification_v1_0_0_alpha_6.py

Paste the contents of this file as a message after uploading the components:

000_LINDA_Generic_Install_Prompt_v1.0.0-alpha.6.txt

a SHA-256 digest.

Do not mix releases. Do not use files with duplicate-download suffixes such as
"(1)", "(2)", or "copy". Each filename must match exactly.

======================================================================
3. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

AWKA provides the Kernel, Definition Registries, immutable Snapshots, event
history, artifact receipts, concurrency controls, and activation process.

LINDA provides the Job Report business model, capabilities, workflow,
validators, renderers, business rules, commands, and controlled fields.

The generic hardened Model contains:

- 84 controlled Job Report fields;
- 4 report sections;
- 7 repeatable record types;
- 6 entities;
- 10 capabilities;
- 9 validators;
- 5 renderers;
- 10 business rules;
- 23 workflow states;
- 26 workflow transitions;
- 8 hardening control areas.

Domain activation creates and freezes LINDA Definition Registries. It does not
create a Job Report. A Job Report is created only after an authorized user runs:

Run New Job Report

======================================================================
4. JOB REPORT STRUCTURE
======================================================================

SECTION 1 - ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT AND WORKING CONDITIONS

The controlled pricing field in this section is named Interpreter Rate Record.

This section records the request, client and end user, setting, purpose,
languages and directions, interpreting modes, delivery configuration, schedule,
breaks, staffing plan, exact interpreter rates, travel terms, safety,
equipment, remote readiness, preparation materials, recording conditions, and
assignment terms.

SECTION 2 - ASSIGNMENT READINESS AND SERVICE DELIVERY

This section records assigned interpreters, coordinator, backup resources,
qualification match, acceptance of working conditions, preparation, protocol
acknowledgment, readiness approval, actual times, actual delivery mode, actual
team, material variances, events or exceptions, and service-delivery status.

SECTION 3 - COMPLETION AND POST-ASSIGNMENT REPORTING

The controlled work-unit field in this section is named Actual Work Units Record.

This section records actual duration, breaks, completion summary, incidents,
actions, linked corrective-action records, debriefing, feedback, client
acceptance, accounting-documentation status, accounting reference, exact days
and hours worked, wellbeing-risk reporting, and restricted follow-up reference.

SECTION 4 - APPROVAL AND RECORD CONTROL

This section records report completion, reviewer approval, review outcome,
final status, controlled-record reference, final-artifact reference, retention
class, related controlled records, and record-control completion.

======================================================================
6. HARDENING RULES
======================================================================

6.1 EVIDENCE CONTROL

Every populated business value must be supported by one of the following:

- an approved Source Packet;
- an authorized human entry;
- a registered controlled default; or
- a registered deterministic derivation.

Unsupported information remains null with a required action. Ambiguous
information remains null under authorized review. LINDA does not guess,
normalize, repair, or infer unsupported business facts.

6.2 HUMAN AND AI IDENTITY SEPARATION

AI agents and tool identities cannot populate human operational fields such as:

- Request Received By;
- Assignment Coordinator;
- Readiness Approver;
- Job Report Completed By; or
- Reviewed / Approved By.

AI and tool identities remain in AWKA technical provenance only.

6.3 FINANCIAL-CALCULATION PROHIBITION

LINDA records only:

- the exact interpreter daily rate;
- the exact interpreter hourly rate;
- currency and rate units exactly as provided;
- optional rate terms or agreement reference;
- exact total days worked; and
- exact total hours worked.

When interpreters have different rates or work units, LINDA records each
interpreter separately.

LINDA must not calculate or display:

- rate multiplied by days;
- rate multiplied by hours;
- extended cost;
- aggregate labor cost;
- overtime cost;
- taxes;
- invoice total; or
- amount payable.

Calculations belong in the linked accounting record or accounting system.

6.4 SECRET MINIMIZATION

Standard Job Report artifacts must not display cleartext:

- passwords;
- meeting access codes;
- access tokens;
- private keys;
- restricted links; or
- other secret credentials.

LINDA displays a controlled reference to the restricted access record instead.

6.5 PRESENTATION SAFETY

Standard artifacts must:

- use human-readable statuses;
- suppress internal machine-status codes;
- use verified glyphs or ASCII-safe hyphens;
- reject replacement glyphs, black squares, and broken bullets;
- display active stage-relevant fields individually; and
- render linked sensitive records as references only.

6.6 EXTERNAL RECORD BOUNDARIES

The Job Report references rather than duplicates:

- resource qualification records;
- interpreting protocols;
- detailed incident and corrective-action records;
- detailed accounting records;
- restricted wellbeing follow-up records; and
- AWKA technical and audit stores.

6.7 CROSS-FIELD RECONCILIATION

Before finalization, LINDA reconciles dates, times, durations, identities,
language teams, interpreter counts, rates, and work units. A blocking conflict
prevents finalization.

6.8 ARTIFACT INTEGRITY

A controlled final artifact requires:

- an immutable selected Snapshot;
- a valid Snapshot SHA-256 digest;
- an eligible pure renderer;
- a final artifact SHA-256 digest;
- an Artifact Receipt created after final hashing; and
- a successful export event.

9. SOURCE PACKETS AND CANDIDATE FACTS
======================================================================

A Source Packet is a registered email, attachment, message, form, or other
source supplied in the current conversation.

When a source is registered, LINDA:

1. assigns a Source Packet ID, such as SRC-001;
2. preserves the source and its metadata;
3. extracts Candidate Facts;
4. records evidence excerpts and proposed mappings;
5. leaves the source pending coordinator review; and
6. does not populate controlled fields before approval.

A Candidate Fact is not automatically canonical. It may be:

- explicit;
- ambiguous;
- derived by a registered rule;
- conflicting; or
- unsupported.

Only supported Candidate Facts from an approved Source Packet may populate
controlled fields. Ambiguous facts remain under review even after the source is
approved.

======================================================================
10. CREATE A NEW JOB REPORT
======================================================================

Upload or paste at least one source, then run:

Run New Job Report

Expected behavior:

- one Job Report Workflow Instance is created;
- the initial source is registered;
- controlled fields remain unchanged until source approval;
- Version 1 Snapshot is created;
- the workflow enters SOURCE_LOADED; and
- LINDA returns the Job Report ID and Source Packet ID.

Do not run this command to activate LINDA. Domain activation and Job Report
creation are separate operations.

======================================================================
11. REGISTER ADDITIONAL SOURCES
======================================================================

Upload or paste the additional evidence, then run:

Register this as an additional Source Packet.

LINDA should assign a new exact ID, such as SRC-002. Review each Source Packet
independently.

======================================================================
12. APPROVE OR REJECT SOURCES
======================================================================

Approve a source using its exact ID:

Approve SRC-001 for field population

Reject a source using its exact ID:

Reject SRC-001

Replace SRC-001 with the actual Source Packet ID.

Rules:

- Vague instructions such as "approve that file" cause no mutation.
- Source approval does not approve ambiguous Candidate Facts.
- A rejected source remains in the audit history.
- Candidate Facts from a rejected source cannot populate fields.
- Approval and rejection require an authorized human actor.

======================================================================
13. UPDATE THE JOB REPORT
======================================================================

After approving evidence or providing an authorized entry, run:

Update Job Report

LINDA validates the change and, if accepted:

- applies supported values;
- preserves prior Snapshots;
- creates a complete successor Snapshot;
- updates field provenance;
- appends events; and
- advances the workflow only when transition conditions are satisfied.

Each update requires the expected prior version and state digest internally.
Stale updates fail without changing the active version.

======================================================================
14. VALIDATE THE CURRENT REPORT
======================================================================

Run:

Validate Current Job Report

Validation checks include:

- source approval and evidence support;
- Candidate Fact status;
- workflow state and required controls;
- 84-field Snapshot completeness;
- repeatable-record integrity;
- required human identities;
- language direction;
- dates, times, duration, and schedule consistency;
- interpreter and team consistency;
- exact rate and work-unit treatment;
- absence of financial calculations;
- secret minimization;
- linked-record boundaries;
- presentation and glyph safety; and
- artifact eligibility.

Possible QA results are:

Pass
Pass with Warnings
Pass with Red Flags
Fail

Validation does not change business facts.

======================================================================
15. FINALIZE THE JOB REPORT
======================================================================

Run:

Finalize Job Report

Finalization is accepted only when all applicable controls pass, including:

- required evidence;
- human actor validity;
- workflow readiness;
- cross-field reconciliation;
- no prohibited financial calculations;
- secret minimization;
- presentation safety;
- linked-record boundaries;
- complete record controls; and
- Snapshot integrity.

If a blocking finding exists, LINDA must reject finalization and leave the prior
active Snapshot unchanged.

3. Run New Job Report.
4. Review the assigned Source Packet ID.
5. Approve or reject the exact Source Packet.
6. Resolve ambiguous facts and missing required information.
7. Update Job Report.
8. Register and approve additional sources as needed.
9. Complete assignment planning and readiness controls.
10. Validate Current Job Report.
11. Record actual service-delivery information after the event.

16. Export the permitted final artifact.

23. COMMON ERRORS AND CORRECTIONS
======================================================================

ERROR: LINDA activation fails because component hashes are pending.
CORRECTION: Replace the preliminary Manifest with the finalized Manifest that
contains exact lowercase SHA-256 hashes and digest_status = verified.

ERROR: Organization placeholders remain unresolved.
CORRECTION: Supply all 12 authorized configuration values and validate them.

ERROR: An AI agent appears in a human coordinator or approver field.
CORRECTION: Replace it with an authorized human or controlled person reference.
Keep the AI identity in technical provenance.

ERROR: The report contains a calculated price or invoice total.
CORRECTION: Remove the calculation. Keep only exact daily/hourly rates and exact
days/hours worked. Reference the accounting record for calculations.

ERROR: A meeting password or access code appears in the report.
CORRECTION: Remove the cleartext value and use the restricted-access record
reference.

ERROR: Language names are present but direction is missing.
CORRECTION: Keep the languages under review and obtain explicit direction. Do
not infer bidirectionality.

ERROR: A vague approval is entered.
CORRECTION: Use the exact Source Packet ID, for example:
Approve SRC-002 for field population

ERROR: Final export exists but no Artifact Receipt is available.
CORRECTION: Regenerate through the registered export Capability, hash the final
artifact, and create the post-hash receipt.

ERROR: Broken bullets, black squares, or replacement glyphs appear.
CORRECTION: Use child tables or ASCII-safe hyphens and rerun presentation
validation before finalization.

ERROR: A historical version needs to become active again.
CORRECTION: Restore it as a new version. Never overwrite existing history.

======================================================================
24. FAIL-CLOSED EXPECTATIONS
======================================================================

LINDA fails closed. When a required check fails, it must not:

- guess missing values;
- silently repair evidence;
- accept unresolved organization placeholders;
- calculate financial totals;
- place AI identities in human fields;
- expose secrets;
- publish partial Definitions;
- commit a failed update;
- claim a successful final export without a receipt; or
- create a Workflow Instance after failed Domain Activation.

The system reports the component, failed check, defect, and required correction.
